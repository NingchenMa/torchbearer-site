How it works

Watch the work, ask why, pass the torch.

One expert, one task, one new hire. TorchBearer turns an ordinary working session into a living Work Map, then coaches the next person with it.

1

Capture

The expert shares their screen and works as usual.
“You moved that one to capex. What made you do that?”TorchBearer, at a natural pause

Silent while they type. Three to five questions per ten minutes, by voice.

2

Map

A short debrief, then a teach-back the expert confirms.
“So: anything over 30 a person needs the approval attached. Did I get that right?”TorchBearer, in the teach-back

Work Map: every step, the reason in the expert's own words, and its guardrails.

3

Teach

A voice tutor on the new hire's own screen.
“Your expert would stop here. Why do you think?”TorchBearer, before the save

Mastery report: what the new hire has learned and what to practise next.
